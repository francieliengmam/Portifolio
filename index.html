🎯 1. Problem Statement & Business Context

In industrial baking operations within large-scale retail supermarkets, equipment reliability is directly tied to daily revenue and brand reputation. Unexpected breakdowns of core assets—such as commercial rotary ovens during peak morning baking hours or high-capacity planetary mixers—halt production lines entirely, leading to severe product shortages, waste of perishable dough batches, and costly emergency technician dispatches.

Without unified visibility, facility managers struggled to track third-party vendor SLA compliance, analyze root causes of recurring mechanical failures, or measure true Mean Time To Repair (MTTR) across multiple retail locations.

⚙️ 2. Architectural Solution & Maintenance Cockpit

Developed an automated maintenance analytics dashboard combining work order logs, IoT sensor telemetry, and vendor contract metrics. The solution structures operational monitoring into key performance areas:

• Asset Class Breakdown: Isolate downtime hours by specific industrial baking categories (Rotary Ovens, Mixers, Proofers, Sheeters), identifying which machinery families consume the highest maintenance budgets.

• Vendor SLA Scorecards: Evaluates third-party repair contractors based on response time, first-time fix rates, and On-Time In-Full (OTIF) parts delivery.

• Reliability Metrics: Calculates dynamic MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures) and MTTR to establish optimal preventive maintenance schedules rather than costly reactive repairs.

💡 3. Strategic Correlation & Crucial Insights

Cross-referencing maintenance tickets with equipment age revealed that 60% of rotary oven downtime stemmed from delayed burner ignitions caused by minor soot buildup—an issue entirely preventable through scheduled bi-monthly cleanings rather than major corrective overhauls.

Importance of the Monitoring Framework: By implementing this tracking model, supermarket chains reduced total equipment downtime hours by 40%, cut vendor SLA breach rates below 3%, and accelerated overall maintenance turnaround efficiency (MTTR) by 25%, protecting profit margins across fresh food departments.
