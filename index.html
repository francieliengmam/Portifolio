🎯 1. Problem Statement & Business Context

In Tier-1 automotive manufacturing, operations are constantly vulnerable to severe discrepancies between long-term OEM demand forecasts (spanning 6 to 12 months) and actual firm EDI (Electronic Data Interchange) purchase orders. When demand volatility is not caught early, plants face catastrophic bottlenecks: unexpected stockouts trigger assembly line shutdowns, while emergency inventory rushes force companies to absorb exorbitant extra freight costs.

⚙️ 2. Architectural Solution & Dashboard Modules

To solve this operational blind spot, I architected a multi-module Power BI command center integrated directly with SAP ERP relational datasets, optimizing OTIF rates, lead times, and fulfillment curves across global OEM accounts.
